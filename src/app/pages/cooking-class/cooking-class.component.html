About the Experience

The flavors of Cartagena — made by you

Gastronomy is a fundamental part of Cartagena's culture and locals take great pride in their Caribbean cuisine. This culinary experience, guided by an expert chef, includes a presentation on key ingredients and a hands-on class where you'll learn to prepare typical dishes like seafood and coconut rice.

Perfect for those who want to savor and recreate Cartagena's flavors at home.

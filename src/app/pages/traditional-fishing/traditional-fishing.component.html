About the Experience

A day on the water with the community

Immerse yourself in local culture with this authentic native fishing tour in La Boquilla, where you'll learn traditional fishing techniques alongside local fishermen and glide through the mangrove lagoon by canoe.

After the day's catch, enjoy the preparation of the fresh food you caught, cooked in the local style. A unique experience that combines adventure, gastronomy and community connection — perfect for nature and food lovers.

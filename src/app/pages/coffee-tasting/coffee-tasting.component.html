About the Experience

From bean to cup — the full story.

In this immersive 2-hour experience, you'll explore the entire arc of Colombian coffee: from cultivation in the mountain highlands and the art of roasting, to the sensory science of tasting. A professional barista guides every step.

You'll finish with a tasting of three distinct processing profiles — Washed, Honey, and Natural — each unlocking a completely different dimension of flavor from the same Colombian bean. A unique window into the specialty coffee of the Caribbean coast.

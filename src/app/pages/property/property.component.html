Our properties are primarily located within Cartagena's Walled City — the most sought-after address in the entire Caribbean. Inside the fortified walls, every cobblestone street and colonial mansion tells five centuries of history, and guests step directly into the city's finest restaurants, boutiques, and plazas.

Getsemaní, once a working-class barrio just outside the walls, has blossomed into the city's most creative quarter — colourful murals, rooftop bars with wall views, and an effortlessly local energy. Bocagrande, by contrast, offers the wide open Caribbean: glass towers, long sandy beaches, and sunset cocktails with nothing but the sea in front of you.

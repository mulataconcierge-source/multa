About the Experience

Five centuries of history in four unforgettable hours

Accompanied by a professional guide, enjoy a full experience exploring Cartagena's most iconic sites. Discover the history of the Historic Center, the art and vibrant life of Getsemaní and be amazed by two of the city's greatest gems.

Choose between the Castillo de San Felipe de Barajas — one of the most formidable Spanish fortresses in the Americas — or the Convento de La Popa, perched high above the city with panoramic views of the entire bay. You pick the one that best matches your interests.

In 1713, the people of Palenque won their freedom — decades before any other enslaved people in the Americas. Their language, music, medicine and spirit survived. Today, you can meet them.
About the Experience

A day full of culture, identity and resilience

Visit San Basilio de Palenque, the first free town in the Americas — a village that preserved its African roots through language, music, medicine and ritual across three centuries.

Accompanied by a local guide, you'll discover its history and Afro-descendant traditions: ancestral hairstyles, music, dance and cuisine — all experienced directly with the people who live and breathe this culture every day.

This is not a museum visit. It's a living community welcoming you in.

Booking Process

01 | Schedule a Meeting

Let's schedule a meeting with your dedicated concierge to discuss your preferences and how we can best assist.

02 | Review our brochure

Before our meeting, we invite you to review our digital brochure, which provides a comprehensive overview of our services and pricing information.

03 | Receive your itinerary

After our meeting, you will receive a personalized itinerary and quote tailored specifically to your needs.

04 | Adjustments & aproval

We will make any necessary adjustments based on your feedback. Once the quote is approved, a 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking and begin arrangements.

*No Initial consultation*

Please note, Mulata does not charge for the initial consultation.

*Deposit for Special Requests*

To proceed with a request that involves an event or requires a more detailed quote not covered in the brochure, a deposit of $50 USD is required. This deposit will be applied toward the concierge fee.

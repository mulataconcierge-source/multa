About the Experience

Timeless charm, on four classic wheels

Discover the timeless charm of Cartagena aboard a beautifully restored vintage car. Cruise through the colorful streets of the Historic Center and the vibrant neighborhood of Getsemaní, watching the walled city unfold around you.

You're accompanied by a knowledgeable local guide who brings the city's rich history, culture and architecture to life at every turn — the stories behind the balconies, the plazas and the colors.
